Treatment planning and how long you may need to stay

The question most people ask before travelling is how many days they will need. Here is how that is decided.

Before you travel

You can send us your question by WhatsApp. We can tell you in general terms what a treatment involves — but we cannot plan your treatment or say how long it will take before an examination. Photographs are not enough for that.

The first appointment

Examination and, where needed, an X-ray. The treatment plan is made after this.

How many days

It depends on the treatment. Some can be completed in a single visit; others, implants in particular, require a healing period between stages. We will tell you the plan for your own situation at the first appointment, before anything begins.

After you go home

If a problem arises after you return, contact us. We are reachable 24 hours. Some situations can be assessed remotely; others need an examination, and in that case seeing a dentist where you are may be the right step. We will say so plainly rather than have you travel unnecessarily.
