## 2. `/en/dental-implants.html`

> **Dental implants**
>
> An implant is a titanium post placed into the jawbone to support a
> replacement tooth. Whether it is suitable depends on the amount and
> quality of bone, gum health and your general medical condition.
>
> **The process usually has two stages.** In the first, the implant is
> placed. The bone then needs time to heal around it — this period
> varies from person to person and is commonly measured in months. In
> the second stage the crown is fitted.
>
> This means most implant treatments **cannot be completed in a single
> visit**. If you are travelling from abroad, plan for either two
> trips or a longer stay. How long applies to you can only be said
> after an examination and an X-ray.
>
> ⚠️ **Tell us before treatment** if you use blood thinners or bone
> medication (such as those taken for osteoporosis), or if you have
> diabetes. These affect how the procedure is planned.
>
> Swelling and discomfort for a few days after the procedure are
> expected and not a sign that something has gone wrong.

## 3. `/en/crowns-and-veneers.html`

⚠️ **Bu sayfa sonuç vaadi sınırına en yakın olan.** Dili bilerek
sade tuttum.

> **Crowns and veneers**
>
> A crown covers a tooth entirely; a veneer covers the front surface.
> Which one is appropriate — or whether either is — depends on how
> much tooth structure remains, the condition of the gum and your bite.
>
> Both usually require more than one appointment: preparation and
> measurement first, then fitting after the laboratory work.
>
> Colour and shape are chosen together with you before the work
> begins. What is achievable differs from person to person and is
> decided at the examination, not in advance.
>
> A crown or veneer does not remove the need for daily cleaning. The
> gum line around them still needs brushing and interdental cleaning.

**Sana sorum:** Kaplama için tipik kaç randevu diyorsun? "more than

## 4. `/en/root-canal-and-general-dentistry.html`

> **Root canal treatment and general dentistry**
>
> Root canal treatment is used when the pulp inside a tooth is
> inflamed or infected. The aim is to keep the tooth rather than
> remove it.
>
> **Pain stopping is not the same as healing.** An infected tooth can
> stop hurting while the infection continues. If your pain went away
> on its own, that is still a reason to be examined.
>
> We also carry out fillings, scaling, extractions and paediatric
> dental care.
>
> A rubber dam is used during root canal treatment. This is standard
> practice, not an optional extra.

**Sana sorum:** Bu sayfada listelediğim işlemler (dolgu, diş taşı

## 5. `/en/treatment-planning-and-your-stay.html`

Bu sayfa hasta turizminin **asıl sorusunu** karşılıyor.

> **Treatment planning and how long you may need to stay**
>
> **Before you travel.** You can send us your question by phone or
> WhatsApp. We can tell you in general terms what a treatment involves
> — but we cannot plan your treatment or say how long it will take
> before an examination. Photographs are not enough for that.
>
> **The first appointment.** Examination and, where needed, an X-ray.
> The treatment plan is made after this.
>
> **How many days.** It depends on the treatment. Some can be
> completed in a single visit; others, implants in particular, require
> a healing period between stages. We will tell you the plan for your
> own situation at the first appointment, before anything begins.
>
> **After you go home.** If a problem arises after you return, contact
> us. We are reachable 24 hours. Some situations can be assessed
> remotely; others need an examination, and in that case seeing a
> dentist where you are may be the right step. We will say so plainly
> rather than have you travel unnecessarily.
