HOME · ROOT CANAL AND GENERAL DENTISTRY

Root canal treatment and general dentistry

Root canal treatment is used when the pulp inside a tooth is inflamed or infected. The aim is to keep the tooth rather than remove it.

Pain stopping is not the same as healing

An infected tooth can stop hurting while the infection continues. If your pain went away on its own, that is still a reason to be examined.

During treatment

A rubber dam is used during root canal treatment. This is standard practice, not an optional extra.

Other treatments

We also carry out fillings, scaling, extractions and paediatric dental care.
