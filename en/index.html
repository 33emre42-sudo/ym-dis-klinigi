24/7 Dental Clinic in Istanbul

We are a dental clinic in the Bağcılar district of Istanbul, open every day, 24 hours. Our clinic is at a single address; we have no other branches.

Who we are

Two dentists work here: Dt. Yunus Emre Çetin and Dt. Mert Daştan. Both are general dentists and carry out all treatments performed at our clinic.

What we do

The pages below explain the treatments carried out at our clinic and how a treatment plan is made.

  • Dental implants
  • Crowns and veneers
  • Root canal treatment and general dentistry

Whether a treatment is suitable for you can only be decided after an examination. We cannot make that assessment from photographs or messages.

We answer the phone in Turkish. If you write to us in English on WhatsApp, we will reply in writing; this lets us read carefully and answer accurately.
