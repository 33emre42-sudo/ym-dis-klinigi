Getting here

Kirazlı Mah. Mevlana Cad. No: 47 D, Bağcılar / Istanbul (34212). This is our only address.

By metro — the easiest way

Kirazlı station is an interchange between two lines: M1B (Yenikapı–Kirazlı) and M3 (Bakırköy Sahil–Kayaşehir).

Leave the station, turn towards Mevlana Caddesi and walk along the street. The clinic is on the street itself, at number 47 D. The walk is short and flat.

From the airport

İstanbul Airport (IST) and Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) both connect to the metro network; from either you can reach Kirazlı by metro with a change. A taxi is the alternative — tell the driver "Kirazlı Metro, Mevlana Caddesi".

At night

When the metro is closed, come by taxi. The clinic is open 24 hours.
